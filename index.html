What we believe

Be yourself.
Stay connected.

TSUNAMI TEAM is built around a simple idea: friendship should never be a cage. It should be a connection that leaves everyone free to move, explore, grow and be themselves. We choose the liquid form because it can adapt without losing its nature, remain calm or become powerful, and connect with what surrounds it.

Freedom

Everyone has their own space, their own choices and their own adventures. Being part of a team does not mean giving up your individuality.

Adaptability

Like water, we can adapt to different situations and different people. We can change our shape without losing who we are.

Connection

The strongest bond is not the one that holds people back, but the one that keeps them connected, wherever life takes them.

One spirit, many adventures

Made of experiences.

We are not defined by one place, one activity or one way of living. We are defined by the experiences we share, the stories we collect and the people who make those moments worth remembering.

Explore

Discover new places, follow new paths and stay curious about what lies beyond the familiar.

Experience

Embrace the unexpected. Every adventure adds something to the person you are becoming.

Share

Some moments become unforgettable because there is someone beside you with whom you can share them.

Reconnect

Everyone is free to follow their own journey. The connection remains, ready to bring us together again.

        Our founding words

        The Statute.

        This is the philosophy behind TSUNAMI TEAM: three ways of being, and a choice to remain free without losing the connection with one another.

        You could be solid, strong as a rock, compact and impenetrable, surely you will be the toughest and the hardest, but only until you remain steady in your place, otherwise you will crumble becoming a punch of sand, losing your certainties like dust in the wind.

        You could be air, free as a bird, light and fast, surely you will enjoy all the amazing experiences that you could have in your thousands trips, but when you’ll turn back your eyes, you won’t find anyone but yourself, and when, at the end of the day, you’ll rest, there will be no one to share with your excited feelings and, if you try to phone someone, maybe he will be busy, like you were since few hours before. And you will be alone, like a wonderful star in the dark space.

        So you could be liquid, clear as water, adaptable and incompressible at the same time, calm and quiet like a crystal mountain lake or stormy and powerful like a tsunami, surely you could enjoy your experiences, you could have your space and making your own adventures with anyone you want, you won’t break any chains, there are none. You could also be yourself with yourself, but the most important thing is that you could be yourself also staying with the others, they will adapt on you and you on them and, at the same time, you won’t lose each other, there will be always a connection, a link, no matter how strong and big, but you know that it will be. And you will be part of something bigger than you, which will lead you to be yourself and greater than yourself. YOU will be US.

        Friendship is liquid, so choose your liquid and be it. TSUNAMI TEAM

        The connection is yours

        Solid is strong, but rigid. Air is free, but it slips away. Liquid is both: as strong as rock when it needs power, as adaptable as air when it needs freedom. That is why there is only one wise choice: be liquid. Stay connected with others, experience life, and be yourself, without ever losing your essence or your freedom.
